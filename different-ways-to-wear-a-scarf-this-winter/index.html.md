# Skinny Scarves Are Back: 7 Ways to Wear a Scarf This Winter

A skinny scarf adds a line of color or shine to a winter outfit without filling the neckline. Tie a narrow burgundy scarf under a blazer, drape a silk ribbon down the front of a knit or wear one as a belt with tailored trousers. If you need a scarf for a cold walk, choose a wider one for the trip and keep the skinny scarf as part of the indoor outfit.

Start with the [seven ways to wear it](#ways) if you already have one. The [fabric and trend guide](#trends) can help you choose between a narrow silk ribbon, a soft knit and a larger scarf for the days when you want more coverage.

## Why the skinny scarf is back

[Cosmopolitan UK](https://www.cosmopolitan.com/uk/fashion/style/a74356476/skinny-scarf-trend/) revisits the long, narrow scarves associated with early-2000s styling and shows ways to move them around an outfit, from the neck to the waist or hair. [Who What Wear’s London trend report](https://www.whowhatwear.com/fashion/winter/london-fashion-trends-winter-2026) treats the skinny scarf as a way to personalize a winter look. You can get that effect from a solid scarf with a visible texture as easily as from a loud print.

The scale makes a difference. A fine silk scarf disappears into a thick turtleneck but stands out against a plain crewneck. A little knitted scarf holds its own over a coat collar. A slightly longer scarf shows beyond the lapels of a blazer. Try it with your jacket on and off to see when the detail will actually be visible.

## Seven ways to wear a scarf this winter

### 1. A low knot with tailoring

Put the scarf around your neck and bring both ends forward. Tie a loose knot near the top button of your blazer and leave one end a little longer. This looks especially good when the scarf and shirt have different textures, like satin over cotton. Tighten the knot only enough to keep it where you want it.

### 2. A loose loop with a knit

Wrap a soft skinny scarf once or twice around the neck and tuck the shorter end into the first loop. Leave a little room between the knit and your neck so it sits naturally. If your sweater has a strong pattern, pick up one of its quieter colors.

### 3. One end thrown over the back

Put the shorter end in front and send the longer end over one shoulder. Check the back in a mirror to see whether the scarf crosses a shoulder seam or hangs straight. Use a short version around a seated dinner where an extra-long end might slip behind the chair.

### 4. An open drape with a coat

Hang both ends in front and let the coat hold the scarf in place. A silk or lightly fringed version can add a line down each lapel. If the coat has a wide collar, try the scarf under it so a small amount shows on each side. Pair a plain scarf with these [winter tartan outfits](https://fashion.allwomenstalk.com/cool-ways-to-wear-the-tartan-trend-this-season/) to keep the print visible.

### 5. A loose belt with trousers

Thread it through two front belt loops or tie it at one hip. A narrow scarf leaves the trouser fastening visible and gives the waist one small point of color. Use a separate belt if you need support from it. This is an easy way to add a little softness to [tailored holiday party trousers](https://fashion.allwomenstalk.com/skip-the-dress-wear-these-trends-to-holiday-parties-instead/).

### 6. A headband with short ends

Fold a fine scarf further if you want a narrow band. Lay it over the crown and tie the ends low at the nape so they sit under the hair. Let a printed section show where the band meets your part. Bring a pin if the fabric tends to shift.

### 7. A ribbon around your ponytail

Secure your ponytail with its usual elastic, then tie the scarf around it. Wear two loose tails with a simple knit or make a small bow for a party. Check the ends against the collar of your coat. For a different wrap, start with these [classic scarf knots](https://fashion.allwomenstalk.com/cute-ways-to-tie-scarves-this-winter/).

## What about warmth?

Cosmopolitan UK and Who What Wear describe a narrow scarf mainly as a styling piece. When the weather calls for more coverage, put a roomy scarf over your coat. Once inside, you can hang up the coat and keep the small scarf at your neck, waist or hair as part of the outfit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are skinny scarves back?

Yes. Cosmopolitan UK and Who What Wear have both featured slim scarves in winter styling. Silk ribbons and narrow knits can give different effects.

### How do you wear a skinny scarf without a bulky knot?

Use a single loose knot or drape both ends. A fine fabric takes up less space at the neckline than a thick knit.

### Which scarves are in style this winter?

Look for skinny scarves, printed shawls, velvet, faux fur, decorative trims and fuller wraps. Choose the shape around the clothes and weather you expect.

### Can you wear a skinny scarf as a belt?

Yes. Tie it at the hip or feed it through some of the belt loops so the ends are visible.

### Does a skinny scarf keep you warm?

A narrow fabric covers a limited area. If you need more coverage outside, bring a wider scarf and use the skinny one to finish the indoor outfit.
