# 7 Iconic Glastonbury Outfits You Can Still Recreate Today

It feels like I’ve been anticipating [Glastonbury Festival](https://www.glastonburyfestivals.co.uk) for years—it’s always one of the first highlights marked on my annual calendar! It isn't just the incredible music, iconic festival atmosphere, or thousands of music lovers happily dancing through the mud. Glastonbury has an unmatched reputation for drawing style icons, and their festival clothing choices never disappoint. If you are searching for the best Glastonbury outfits and timeless festival style inspiration, these classic celebrity looks remain effortlessly wearable today.

- Statement outerwear, from vibrant printed bombers to structured blazers

- Weather-proof footwear like sturdy biker boots and muddy wellies

- Eye-catching headwear, including oversized fedoras and retro beanies

## 1. Cara Delevingne…

![Cara Delevingne…](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/07/1_cara-delevingne_600x832.jpg)

Cara went back to her classic street-style look for Glastonbury 2013, pairing dark skinny jeans with black sneakers, a cozy gray beanie hat, and an unforgettable statement jacket. Her colorful floral printed bomber was a Helen Steele design, and sales surged right after Cara was spotted wearing it. She showed how effortless festival clothing can be, staying low-key and comfortable while showcasing a bold pop of artistic color amid the muddy festival grounds. Love.

## 2. Sienna Miller…

![Sienna Miller…](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/07/2_sienna-miller_595x932.jpg)

Sienna Miller proves that boho chic remains the ultimate festival aesthetic. From her tousled waves to that wide-brimmed cowboy hat, bold oriental blazer, black shorts, and trusty biker boots, Sienna looked relaxed, comfortable, and completely stylish. Pairing structured outerwear with casual denim shorts creates a balanced festival outfit. If you love incorporating [floral pattern designs](#1) into your wardrobe, Sienna's eclectic layering offers the perfect moodboard.

## 3. Kate Moss…

![Kate Moss…](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/07/3_kate-moss_382x572.jpg)

Kate Moss, the reigning queen of cool, kept her festival fashion chic and unaffected to such an extent that even her laid-back expression works as an accessory. She combined rich textures by mixing shearling and leather, adding an instant rock-and-roll edge with knee-high black leather boots. When planning your own woman Glastonbury outfits, remember that oversized dark sunnies will always carry the day through any unpredictable festival weather.

## 4. Carey Mulligan…

![Carey Mulligan…](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/07/4_casey-mulligan_600x813.jpg)

Carey Mulligan attended Glastonbury to support her husband Marcus Mumford of [Mumford & Sons](https://www.mumfordandsons.com), bringing a sophisticated, minimalist take to festival attire. Her outfit featured black ripped jeans and a relaxed black tee—simple pieces that fit right into the crowd. Adding a tailored black blazer provided an understated touch of elegance, beautifully complemented by vintage-inspired retro shades.

## 5. Cressida Bonas…

![Cressida Bonas…](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/07/5_cressida-bonas_600x846.jpg)

Cressida Bonas channeled a playful 1990s throwback vibe at Glastonbury, demonstrating how retro trends can feel fresh and carefree. With classic denim dungarees, a bright retro scrunchie, and neon-rimmed sunglasses, she captured the fun-loving spirit of festival fashion. To rock a similar look, keep your outfit playful and relaxed, just like Cressida's nostalgic ensemble.

## 6. Florence Welch…

![Florence Welch…](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/07/6_florence-welch_600x877.jpg)

Florence Welch is the master of bohemian festival dressing, carrying off ethereal wardrobe choices with complete confidence. During the festival, she paired a fringed monochrome jacket with slim jeans, a crisp white blouse, and a statement cowboy belt for day one, switching to a printed shift dress for day two. Complemented by round sunglasses and flowing red hair, her style felt wonderfully light and carefree. Much like the [knee-high leather boots](#3) favored by Kate Moss, Florence's fringed layers add movement and texture to festival clothes.

## 7. Alexa Chung…

![Alexa Chung…](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/07/7_alexa-chung_600x812.jpg)

Alexa Chung mastered understated elegance for Glastonbury 2013, elevating simple dark skinny jeans with a sharp camel pea coat and a rich burgundy oversized fedora. Charming braided pigtails and a bright smile gave the outfit a friendly, approachable feel, while iconic [Hunter wellies](https://www.hunterboots.com) provided practical protection against the Glasto mud. This look remains one of the best examples of how to dress smartly for festival weather.

The celebs were definitely keen to get noticed at Glastonbury 2013—Millie Mackintosh opted for a black crop top, green oversized shirt, and leather shorts, while Poppy Delevingne looked amazing in denim shorts and a big feathery headdress. Key takeaways for creating memorable festival outfits include:

- Balance statement outerwear with simple, comfortable base layers.

- Incorporate personality with retro accessories like scrunchies, hats, or round sunnies.

- Choose practical footwear like wellies or sturdy boots for muddy fields.
