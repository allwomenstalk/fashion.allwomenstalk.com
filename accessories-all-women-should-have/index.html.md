# 7 Essential Accessories Every Woman Should Have in Her Closet

There are definitely core accessories every woman should have in her wardrobe rotation. Why? Because staple accessories go effortlessly with almost anything, making daily outfit planning seamless while bringing functional convenience to our lives. Accessories are the ultimate finishing touch that transforms a simple outfit into a polished ensemble. From timeless jewelry to functional everyday bags, discovering the essential accessories every woman needs will elevate your style without requiring a complete wardrobe overhaul. Here are seven recommendations for classic accessories every woman should have in her collection.

## 1. Sunglasses

![Sunglasses](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/27205840_4726_500x749.jpg)

Sunglasses are one of the top accessories every woman should have. Any true fashion enthusiast knows quality shades can be worn all throughout the year to protect your eyes and complete your look. For example, paired with a sleek leather jacket and ankle boots, oversized sunglasses look strikingly edgy in the fall. Furthermore, wearing classic frame sunglasses with a structured pea coat on sunny winter afternoons screams sophisticated style. Investing in a pair with full UV protection from classic eyewear brands like [Ray-Ban](https://www.ray-ban.com) ensures long-lasting eye health alongside timeless aesthetic appeal.

## 2. Wristlet

![Wristlet](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/27210059_4684_500x750.jpg)

It’s a night out on the town with your friends and the last thing you want to worry about is hauling an oversized purse. Rather than carrying a bulky bag, substitute a sleek wristlet! Wristlets are convenient, portable, and affordable. Plus, most contemporary wristlets are designed big enough to carry essential items like your cell phone, lipstick, keys, cards, and cash. If you prefer not to carry a heavier [casual purse](#3) for quick evening outings, a wristlet is the ideal lightweight alternative. Just remember to empty out your wristlet the morning after so you don’t forget to move your credit card or your car keys back to your main bag.

## 3. Casual Purse

![Casual Purse](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/27205932_3391_500x750.jpg)

A casual, or “daytime”, purse is a must for all women. This is the versatile bag that you can take to work every day, stuff your lunch or spare clothes in, and comfortably carry anywhere. The ideal casual purse is roomy on the inside, slouchy (not stiff) so you can fit things like spare shoes or an umbrella inside, and offers flexible carrying options.

- **Spacious Storage:** Accommodates daily essentials, electronics, and unexpected extras.
- **Dual Strap Design:** Includes both short top handles and a detachable long shoulder strap.
- **Durable Material:** Soft, supple leather or canvas that holds up to daily wear.

## 4. Phone Case with a Screen Protector

![Phone Case with a Screen Protector](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/27210340_7916_500x750.jpg)

Personally, I find “naked” phones look sleek, but most modern smartphones are fragile and prone to accidental damage. Therefore, to protect your technology investment, you should definitely purchase a protective phone case. Furthermore, using a case alongside a tempered glass screen protector (both can be purchased individually or together) offers total drop defense. Industry-leading protective brands like [OtterBox](https://www.otterbox.com) provide durable slim cases that shield your device without unnecessary bulk. In addition, keeping your phone in pristine condition is in your best interest if you choose to sell or trade in your device in the future.

| Sunglasses | Year-round UV protection & style | Classic frames with full UV shielding |
| Wristlet | Evening outings & light travel | Compact size for essential items |
| Casual Purse | Workdays & daily commuting | Spacious interior with dual straps |
| Phone Case | Daily device protection | Shock absorption & screen safety |

## 5. Pearl Earrings

![Pearl Earrings](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/27210457_2237_500x750.jpg)

First, they don’t need to be real pearl earrings! High-quality imitation pearl studs offer the exact same elegant aesthetic. I recommend pearl studs because they are a classic accessory that is always appropriate for any occasion at any age. Pearl earrings can be worn for formal occasions like job interviews or cocktail parties. In addition, they can be worn during the daytime alongside a classic [watch](#6) to add a classy detail to an otherwise casual outfit!

## 6. Watch

Every woman needs a watch, whether it is for show or for actual timekeeping! Watches are one of the essential accessories every woman needs to anchor her wristwear. They are wonderful to wear alone for a minimalist statement or layered alongside delicate bangles. Brands like [Nordstrom](https://www.nordstrom.com) feature wide selections of quality fashion timepieces. However, if you do wear a watch with other wrist jewelry, make sure the metal tone of your watch matches your accessories. For example, avoid mixing a bright gold watch with silver bangles for a cleaner, unified look.

## 7. Flats

![Flats](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/27210511_0623_500x661.jpg)

Quality flats are just as comfortable as slip-ons or sneakers and can be just as dressy as heels. Personally, I think flats are among the most important must-have accessories because shoes can make or break an outfit, and the right pair can send your style over the top. Stylish flats are affordable if you shop at the right stores, making it easy to own a few casual flats for the day and dressy pairs for the evening. When paired with comfortable outerwear and dark [sunglasses](#1), flats complete a chic off-duty outfit. The best part of flats? There’s no risk of breaking your ankle the way there is when wearing high heels!

When I was younger, I thought fashion was all about having the right clothes for each season. Little did I know that the secret to a great outfit was great accessories! Plus, the best accessories double as practical items that make life more convenient, like the perfect slouchy bag or a cute wristlet. What are some of your must-have accessories?
