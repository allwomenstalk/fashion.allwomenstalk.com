# 7 Street Style Trends for Spring: Classic Looks to Elevate Your Outfit

Street style trends offer endless inspiration for updating your wardrobe every season. Beyond the runway presentations at [fashion weeks](https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows), everyday street style from editors, bloggers, and fashion enthusiasts frequently defines what we actually wear. Whether you are refreshing your daily outfits or building a capsule wardrobe, these seven classic street style trends showcase how to mix comfort, structure, and personal flare effortlessly.

## 1. Chic Sweaters

![Chic Sweaters](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/03/1-Chic-Sweaters_500x750.jpg)

Sweaters are a perennial street style favorite when structured thoughtfully. Forget oversized, shape-losing knits; modern statement sweaters feature clean lines, neoprene blends, or bold graphic patterns. A well-fitted sweater instantly elevates a relaxed daytime outfit while keeping you warm during transitional weather.

Here are a few ways to style statement knits:

- Pair graphic prints with sleek mini skirts or tailored shorts.
- Layer structured sweaters over [sheer skirts](#7) for high-contrast texture.
- Complete the outfit with clean ankle boots or modern minimalist jewelry.

## 2. Matching Sets

![Matching Sets](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/03/2-Matching-Sets_550x834.jpg)

Coordinated sets remain one of the easiest ways to look put-together with minimal effort. Choosing matching separates in bold colors, prints, or subtle monochrome tones creates a polished, high-fashion aesthetic. A tailored shorts suit offers a chic, casual look, while a matching top and midi skirt combination delivers effortless elegance.

## 3. Polka Dots

![Polka Dots](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/03/3-Polka-Dots_550x824.jpg)

Polka dots bring timeless charm and playfulness to spring fashion. From subtle micro-dots to striking oversized spots, this retro pattern adds personality to simple silhouettes. A classic polka dot blouse pairs effortlessly with denim and sandals for weekend outings, or you can opt for a head-to-toe spotted dress for a bold fashion statement.

| Trend | Key Feature | Best Styling Pair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chic Sweaters | Graphic prints & structured shape | Mini skirts, tailored shorts, or ankle boots |
| Matching Sets | Coordinated monochrome or pattern | Tailored shorts suit or top and skirt set |
| Polka Dots | Classic retro print in varied scales | Straight-leg denim or neutral accessories |

## 4. Over-the-Shoulder Jackets

![Over-the-Shoulder Jackets](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/03/4-Over-The-Shoulder-Jackets_600x900.jpg)

Draping a coat or blazer over your shoulders without putting your arms through the sleeves creates an instant editorial silhouette. This simple styling trick gives tailored outerwear a relaxed, confident feel while offering lightweight protection against cooler spring breezes. It works exceptionally well over [matching sets](#2) or structured dresses.

## 5. Ripped Jeans

![Ripped Jeans](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/03/5-Ripped-Jeans_550x824.jpg)

Distressed denim remains a staple for effortless off-duty outfits. Slouchy, faded, or cuffed ripped jeans add texture and edge to any wardrobe. To keep the look balanced rather than overly casual, team distressed denim from classic brands like [Levi's](https://www.levi.com) with a sharp blazer, crisp button-down blouse, and pointed heels.

## 6. Sneakers

![Sneakers](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/03/6-Sneakers_500x750.jpg)

Athletic footwear continues to dominate street style, combining practicality with streetwear cool. Slipping on low-top leather trainers or retro running shoes from heritage labels like [Adidas](https://www.adidas.com) grounds feminine pieces with casual utility. Contrast sporty kicks with distressed [ripped jeans](#5) or tailor-cut trousers for a balanced daily look.

Popular sneaker choices for street style:

- Retro running sneakers with neutral color blocking.
- Minimalist white leather low-tops.
- High-top canvas trainers paired with cropped denim.

## 7. Sheer Skirts

![Sheer Skirts](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2013/03/7-Sheer-Skirts_500x750.jpg)

Bring lightness and dimension to your spring wardrobe with sheer fabrics. Delicately lacy skirts or transparent mesh overlays add movement and texture. For an approachable, wearable outfit, style a sheer skirt with a tonal slip underneath and pair it with a structured top to balance the delicacy of the fabric.

Street style blogs and social platforms make it easy to find fresh outfit inspiration every day. Incorporating these classic trends into your wardrobe allows you to experiment with new textures, silhouettes, and patterns while maintaining your personal style. Which of these street style trends are you excited to try this spring?
