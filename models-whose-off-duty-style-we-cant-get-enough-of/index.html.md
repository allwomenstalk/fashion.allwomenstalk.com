# 9 Iconic Models Whose Off\-Duty Style We Can't Get Enough Of

We pay attention to the clothes that models wear on the runways, but nowadays their off-duty style captures just as much notice. No sooner are they off the runways than they are once again being snapped by street style photographers who can’t get enough of their candid fashion choices. Being impeccably dressed seems like a 24/7 job for a professional model. Certain style icons consistently hit the nail on the head when crafting a signature model off duty look. Take a look at these models whose off duty style we can’t seem to get enough of.

## 1. Jourdan Dunn

![Jourdan Dunn](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/hj/lc/05/atbvkngw_600x917.jpg)

Kicking off our list of models' off duty style is Jourdan Dunn. While the term “model off duty” might have you thinking of casually disheveled looks, Jourdan’s style is anything but. Even her most casual outfits seem to have an overall polished effect. She manages to mix classic wardrobe foundations with trend-based statement pieces, making her everyday wardrobe one worth following closely for fashion inspiration.

## 2. Cara Delevingne

![Cara Delevingne](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/yu/0q/sr/lio1tffg_500x669.jpg)

Her outfits might be high fashion on the runways, but when she’s off the clock, Cara Delevingne’s outfits are strictly streetwear. Her off-duty outfits have a relaxed edge and they always look comfortable to boot. Featured in leading fashion publications like [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com), she is never afraid to rock bold prints, graphic tees, or eye-catching patterns. She’s hardly ever seen without a funky beanie or statement hat to top off her streetwear ensemble.

Key elements of Cara's street style approach include:

- Relaxed graphic hoodies and tees
- Funky knit beanies and caps
- Comfortable high-top sneakers

## 3. Hanne Gaby Odiele

![Hanne Gaby Odiele](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/go/0h/tu/0ip66b7t_465x700.jpg)

Hanne Gaby Odiele’s personal style is exceptionally fashion-forward. She’s not afraid of pushing aesthetic boundaries, and her off-duty outfits seamlessly blend utilitarian elements with quirky, off-beat looks. She possesses that rare ability to turn an eclectic mish-mash of current trends into an effortlessly put-together ensemble.

## 4. Miranda Kerr

![Miranda Kerr](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ra/jb/cu/c1zxgq0s_600x872.jpg)

As a former [Victoria’s Secret](https://www.victoriassecret.com) Angel, Miranda Kerr has become a major off-duty style icon. Her outfits are always smart and polished, whether she is stepping out in sleek off duty leather pants paired with a tailored blouse or wearing a fitted pencil dress. For a sharp alternative to [Jourdan Dunn's polished aesthetic](#1), Miranda Kerr offers contemporary elegance with a distinctly feminine flair.

## 5. Liu Wen

![Liu Wen](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wi/t3/h1/rxfar4bn_480x648.jpg)

If there’s one word to describe Liu Wen’s off duty style, it would be "sleek." Think tailored trousers, crisp collared shirts, chic flat shoes, and roomy tote bags perfect for a day of traveling between runway shows. Her ensembles are easy to recreate with minimalist staples you likely already own in your closet. Who needs attention-grabbing accessories when you know how to master the basics? Liu Wen’s outfits represent understated style at its finest.

Here is a breakdown of core off-duty style formulas:

| Style Formula | Signature Items | Key Aesthetic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polished Minimalist | Crisp collared shirt, tailored trousers | Sleek & Functional |
| Streetwear Edge | Graphic hoodie, statement beanie | Casual & Comfortable |
| Edgy Glam | Off duty leather pants, tailored blazer | Modern & Chic |

## 6. Karlie Kloss

![Karlie Kloss](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/a1/ld/rf/lgugv36e_592x888.jpg)

She’s a chameleon on the catwalk, but off duty Karlie Kloss’s outfits are usually relaxed, clean, and feminine. You are just as likely to see her wearing elegant, sophisticated dresses as you are finding her in classic skinny jeans and a simple white t-shirt.

## 7. Jess Hart

![Jess Hart](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/0c/o7/n5/vbqsddaa_518x917.jpg)

She might not be the single biggest household name, but if you follow street style photography, you have likely spotted this Australian model’s face. Her style is fresh and varied, making her off-duty choices consistently interesting. She can just as easily rock an edgy leather look as she can a floaty bohemian dress or a tailored blazer with structured trousers.

Her versatile wardrobe relies on three core staples:

- Tailored blazers and trousers
- Floaty bohemian dresses
- Edgy leather jackets

## 8. Rosie Huntington-Whiteley

![Rosie Huntington-Whiteley](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ba/g6/n6/s3aw33dt_541x794.jpg)

Rosie Huntington-Whiteley’s off-duty model look has a distinct bohemian edge. Frequently highlighted by major fashion outlets like [Harper's Bazaar](https://www.harpersbazaar.com), she often sports the classic model uniform of dark skinny jeans and a leather jacket. However, her outfits are never without bohemian touches, and she is well known for pairing sleek leather layers with retro-inspired prints. Much like [Miranda Kerr's sleek leather pants look](#4), Rosie proves how versatile leather staples can be.

## 9. Kate Moss

![Kate Moss](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/dy/gy/ap/jje22zti_600x906.jpg)

Kate Moss is the ultimate quintessential icon of model off-duty style, and no roundup would be complete without her. She is constantly photographed by street style photographers and paparazzi alike, resulting in endless inspiration from her off-duty outfits. Her signature formula typically consists of fitted skinny jeans paired with an effortless mix of high-end designer pieces and vintage finds. Throw in a leopard print coat and a floppy fedora, and you have the definitive Moss outfit, echoing [Rosie Huntington-Whiteley's bohemian edge](#8).

Sure, high-profile models might have access to designer wardrobes, but you will also find that top models frequently wear thrifted finds and accessible chain store buys. Their style is remarkably accessible compared to high-fashion runway looks. Which model's off-duty style do you love most?
