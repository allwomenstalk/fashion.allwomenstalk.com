# 25 Most Stylish Olympic Teams & Olympic Uniform Styles Ranked

With the involvement of top world-class designers, the Entrance of the Athletes in the Olympic Opening Ceremony has evolved into a major runway showcase alongside the world's greatest sporting event. From high-fashion Olympic uniform trends to iconic team aesthetics, athletes showcased stunning creations from global fashion powerhouses like [Ralph Lauren](https://www.ralphlauren.com), [Stella McCartney](https://www.stellamccartney.com), [Lacoste](https://www.lacoste.com), and [Christian Louboutin](https://www.christianlouboutin.com) alongside premier home-country designers. From tailored blazers to vibrant traditional attire, this showcase brought unforgettable opening ceremony style and olympics hot girl style to the world stage. Let’s explore the most memorable team uniforms and see which country took home the gold in runway elegance!

- **High-Fashion Collaborations:** Renowned luxury labels crafted formal parade attire for athletes.
- **Cultural Heritage:** Traditional garments brought vibrant national pride to the global stage.
- **Athletic Modernity:** Contemporary activewear met tailored ceremony blazers.

| United States | Preppy Classic | Tailored [Ralph Lauren](https://www.ralphlauren.com) navy blazers |
| Great Britain | Athletic Elegance | Union Jack pattern by [Stella McCartney](https://www.stellamccartney.com) |

## 1. Canada

![person, red, crowd, human action, marching,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cz/hg/id86dat957aeed76b911f829840685_600x381.jpg)

Canada turned national pride up to an 11 with these bold and beautiful maple leaf jackets designed by Dsquared2. The oversized maple leaf emblem on the back created an unmissable silhouette during the parade. There was absolutely no mistaking which country these athletes represented as they marched through the stadium in style.

## 2. Australia

![person, profession, uniform, military uniform,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/k9/zj/qbo72x0r57aeed76d7586281712207_600x338.jpg)

Australia displayed their playful and cheerful spirit in pinstriped green blazers paired with crisp white shorts and skirts. Reminiscent of classic vintage prep, the tailored design was clean, cheerful, and crisp. Much like the structured aesthetic seen in [South Korea's sharp blazers](#21), Australia managed to strike a memorable balance between formal ceremony attire and relaxed summer vibes.

## 3. Jamaica

![person, yellow, marching, bandy, ーーー,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qc/oj/da7ud2ox57aeed7710183813043080_600x400.jpg)

The vibrant colors of Jamaica are instantly recognizable at the Olympic Games, and the team’s outfits radiated Caribbean pride. Featuring rich yellow jackets, deep green accents, and sleek black trousers, the collection exuded energy and confidence on the parade track.

## 4. Cameroon

![person, outerwear,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rh/p2/vcjooska57aeed777d79c835931352_600x338.jpg)

The Cameroonian delegation looked ready for a high-fashion runway as they stepped into the stadium. Sporting traditional patterned garments with intricate embroidery and bold color palettes, their attire was one of the undisputed stylistic highlights of the opening ceremony.

## 5. Croatia

![sports, crowd, cheerleading,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/r3/rg/sc2of5q957aeed77d61ee352191487_600x400.jpg)

The iconic red-and-white checkerboard motif of Croatia is always instantly recognizable in world sports. The team looked exceptionally sharp in clean jackets featuring signature geometric panels, making for a smart, graphic, and super stylish entrance.

## 6. Hungary

![Hungary](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/z6/u1/za535leg57aeed78051d1356246646_600x338.jpg)

Hungary introduced a modern design featuring vertical stripes in national red, white, and green. While some fashion commentators noted that the high-contrast linear print resembled modern graphic barcode patterns, the overall look was sleek, contemporary, and eye-catching.

## 7. United States of America

![crowd, cheering, people, audience, sports,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/tq/nn/w6ujjuwp57aeed7a553d5257449499_600x400.jpg)

Michael Phelps and Team USA looked effortlessly cool in their classic red, white, and navy ensembles. Designed by [Ralph Lauren](https://www.ralphlauren.com), the dark blazers, striped boat shoes, and illuminated logo jackets gave the delegation an iconic preppy aesthetic.

## 8. Indonesia

![musician, costume, santa claus, iae,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/m4/fp/f5psda2g57aeed7a519d0570433030_600x338.jpg)

The Indonesian team easily ranked among the most regal delegations of the evening. Dressed in magnificent traditional royal costumes featuring ornate red-and-gold headpieces and elaborate capes, their arrival was a magnificent celebration of national heritage.

## 9. Barbados

![sports, marching, bandy, winter sport, musician,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mm/gf/c1l31zgm57aeed7a670f2483706992_600x404.jpg)

The delegation from Barbados made a sunny statement in bright yellow blazers paired with dark trousers and skirts. While some viewers joked about the sharp uniform appearance, the vibrant yellow shade brought unmistakable tropical warmth to the arena.

## 10. Lesotho

![education, community, costume, festival, LESOTO,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mu/jx/yv6vt3fc57aeed7a3cbfb884185309_600x338.jpg)

Traditional Basotho conical hats (Mokorotlo) gave Lesotho’s athletic tracksuits a distinctive cultural touch. Paired with woven blankets over their shoulders, the outfit combined sporty practicality with deeply authentic national identity.

## 11. Mozambique

![MOCAMBIQUE,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/te/fy/mn8lr8ld57aeed7b8c383060363836_600x338.jpg)

Mozambique presented a warm neutral palette featuring tailored beige jackets and patterned accents. While some commentators drew quirky comparisons, fans of classic tailored outerwear appreciated the sophisticated earth-toned aesthetic.

## 12. Norway

![cheering, art, crowd, people, festival,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/l1/tj/jogvfs3s57aeed7cdcd51163600339_600x400.jpg)

Norway chose dynamic zip-up jackets adorned with intricate geometric paint prints in national colors. The playful optical pattern made the athletes stand out vividly in the parade crowd.

## 13. Pakistan

![clothing, costume, modern dance, dance,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/eq/gk/jssd2nsh57aeed7d12bd9633125319_600x338.jpg)

The Pakistani team showcased classic grace in crisp white sherwanis and traditional attire. Combining elegant tailoring with supreme comfort, this pristine look was a standout highlight of cultural formal wear.

## 14. Great Britain

![sports, athlete, combat sport, sport venue, energy drink,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/nu/ia/fxaqxdnk57aeed7d3fa42469825267_600x407.jpg)

Created by acclaimed designer [Stella McCartney](https://www.stellamccartney.com), Team GB’s outfits incorporated coat-of-arms crests and a harmonious mix of navy, white, and red. Much like [Ralph Lauren's sharp tailoring for Team USA](#7), McCartney delivered modern athletic sophistication for Great Britain's athletes.

## 15. Portugal

![marching, festival, File, aHiliLE,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/xp/i3/veul3amb57aeed7d59e52503311010_600x338.jpg)

Portugal opted for a stylishly relaxed ensemble featuring dark denim jeans paired with tailored blazers and red polo shirts. The casual approach felt effortless and modern, proving that denim can belong on an Olympic stadium floor.

## 16. Denmark

![carnival, festival, DINAMARCA,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fi/d4/bhoyqn3k57aeed7e07fc5855975872_600x406.jpg)

Denmark embraced refined Scandinavian minimalism with rich red blazers and clean white trousers. Tennis star Caroline Wozniacki led the delegation, perfectly modeling the elegant silhouette.

## 17. Sudan

![person, human action, sports, physical exercise, martial arts,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/yx/kp/b82kzvrp57aeed7e1a691933166015_600x338.jpg)

Sudan brought timeless traditional elegance to the arena in pristine white thobes and flowing robes. The crisp lines and graceful garments provided a striking visual contrast to modern sportswear.

## 18. Sweden

![yellow, bandy, lee,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/d2/hg/xdaw8aog57aeed7f5e0f6946948031_600x338.jpg)

Sweden collaborated with global high-street giant [H&M](https://www.hm.com) for activewear outfits in vibrant yellow and blue recycled fabrics. In contrast to [Portugal's casual denim looks](#15), Sweden prioritized sporty performance gear for their parade wardrobe.

## 19. Malaysia

![toy,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mf/gp/ve8i1x5957aeed7fd95b5401560337_600x379.jpg)

Malaysia made a vivid impact in bright orange jackets featuring tiger-stripe motifs. The radiant color combination energized the parade track and ensured the delegation commanded attention.

## 20. Tuvalu

![social group, crowd, festival, carnival, people,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qu/ds/qwmbqdde57aeed7fd4c25071700586_600x400.jpg)

The close-knit Tuvalu delegation wore traditional handcrafted floral headpieces (fou) paired with ocean-blue ceremonial wear, bringing island warmth and authentic Pacific heritage to the global audience.

## 21. South Korea

![sports, award, medal, bronze medal, REUTERS,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qp/uz/lc15o1ae57aef2b5716c5211238732_600x402.jpg)

South Korea introduced crisp navy blazers and white trousers infused with insect-repelling technology for athlete comfort. The sharp tailoring and polished accessories looked smart and classic.

## 22. South Africa

![art, winter sport, dance, sports, figure skating,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/bh/zt/e3wde9gr57aef25ee5b0b551839314_600x396.jpg)

South Africa kept things sporty and unified in classic green and gold tracksuits with matching athletic footwear, proudly displaying national athletic pride on the Rio stadium stage.

## 23. Montenegro

![person, human action,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/v7/hs/m7ctyv4a57aef332891d3615153394_600x430.jpg)

Montenegro turned heads in light blue blazers and pale boater hats, prompting whimsical comparisons to wizarding academy uniforms while maintaining a charming vintage aesthetic.

## 24. Brazil

![sports, winter sport, figure skating,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/r9/i1/q2piv9q757aef59178ab9975561119_600x383.jpg)

As host nation, Brazil brought tropical flair in outfits featuring vibrant rainforest leaf prints designed by Lenny Niemeyer. Combined with classic navy blazers, the look celebrated Brazilian biodiversity and hospitality.

## 25. Tonga

![toy, sports,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ek/la/zd8lwer557aeed8035ea6397545349_600x338.jpg)

Saving the most viral moment for last, Tonga flag bearer Pita Taufatofua became an instant global sensation as he entered the stadium wearing traditional taʻovala attire and coconut oil. His proud representation of Tongan culture stole the show and became one of the most memorable opening ceremony entrances in Olympic history.
