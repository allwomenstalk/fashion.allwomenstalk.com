# 7 Chic Ways to Wear Chevron Print This Spring

Are you wondering what would be some great ways to wear chevron print this spring? Chevron print is hot right now, and it seems like you see it everywhere you look. From breezy dresses to chic everyday accessories, this iconic zigzag pattern adds instant energy to any outfit. Let’s talk about some of the most fashionable ways to wear chevron print so you can decide which option fits your personal style best.

## 1. A Chevron Maxi

![A Chevron Maxi](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/0i/du/k3al27bp_500x999.jpg)

Chevron maxis are one of the most popular ways to wear chevron print this spring. You can find them at so many different places. I even saw several different colors of chevron maxis at my local [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com). All you have to do is pair it with a solid top and comfortable sandals, and you have a great spring outfit. You can wear this versatile combo in so many different situations and settings, from casual weekend brunches to afternoon strolls. If you prefer lighter accessories instead of a full outfit, you can also opt for a cozy [chevron scarf](#6) to complement your look.

## 2. A Chevron Tunic

![A Chevron Tunic](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/t3/zy/vb5serq8_600x899.jpg)

The chevron tunic is also very fashionable and easy to layer. You can wear it with leggings or your favorite skinny jeans for a sleek, comfortable silhouette. It is an outfit that can easily be worn at the office or out on the town for dinner with friends. This is an option I have been particularly tempted to purchase. I think a chevron tunic would be a very versatile item to keep in your closet for quick, stylish outfits.

- Pair bold zigzag tunics with solid neutral bottoms to keep the pattern balanced.
- Opt for breathable fabrics so your tunic stays comfortable all day long.
- Add minimalist jewelry to let the chevron pattern take center stage.

## 3. Chevron Nails

![Chevron Nails](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/zz/zg/ogqyzd8t_500x759.jpg)

Chevron nails are extremely popular this season. It is easy to see why, too. It is an easy and economical way to wear chevron without committing to a full wardrobe overhaul. It is also a great way to try this trend out to see if you like it before buying clothes in the pattern. Chevron nails are also something you can do yourself at home; it really isn’t complicated with nail tape or stencils. For starters, try this design out on an accent nail, or browse design ideas on [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com) for inspiration.

## 4. A Chevron Purse

![A Chevron Purse](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rz/ay/pz76ovbi_587x900.jpg)

Chevron purses are popping up everywhere in stores and online. It actually makes me a little sad because I have already purchased a purse for spring and don’t need another one right now. But if I did, I would certainly be getting a chevron purse! They are so cute, eye-catching, and fashionable. They are also a great choice for a woman of any age to carry for work or weekend outings. You can find stylish options at major retailers like [Target](https://www.target.com), and they look extra chic when paired with subtle [chevron nail art](#3).

## 5. A Chevron Tank

![A Chevron Tank](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ak/jf/ep366arw_450x600.jpg)

A tank top with a chevron design is a great way to wear chevron this spring. It is a relaxed, casual option for warmer weather. You can wear a chevron tank with shorts, a denim skirt, or even a classic pair of jeans. I actually purchased one in coral for myself and one in bright yellow for my daughter, and we can’t wait to wear them all season long.

| Chevron Style | Best Occasion | Recommended Pairing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maxi Dress | Weekend Outings | Solid Top & Sandals |
| Scarf | Cool Mornings | Basic Tee & Jeans |
| Bathing Suit | Beach & Pool Days | Sun Hat & Cover-Up |

## 6. A Chevron Scarf

![A Chevron Scarf](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/hr/fb/iyhces9o_600x900.jpg)

Chevron scarves are a wonderful way to wear this fashionable print without spending a lot of money on the trend. I have seen them available for as low as five dollars. All you need to do is pair one with a simple white t-shirt and your favorite jeans, and you are good to go. It is also a great option for those cool spring mornings when you don’t know exactly what to wear. It gives you a fresh spring look with just a little bit of extra warmth.

## 7. A Chevron Bathing Suit

There are a lot of options in chevron print bathing suits this year. I was actually quite surprised to see how many different chevron bathing suits are available online and in resort shops. It is definitely the print to wear to the beach or pool this year. This print is so trendy that it will be attention-grabbing and help direct attention away from any body flaws you might be self-conscious about. All anyone will see is the stylish, fun suit you are sporting. If you want to test the pattern in smaller doses before diving into swimwear, start with a cute [chevron purse](#4) instead.

These are 7 different ways that you can wear chevron print this spring. How do you plan to wear this very popular design this season? I can’t wait to let your ideas inspire me!
