# Velvet Flats Are Winter’s Chicest Shoe: How to Wear Them

Velvet flats can dress up winter trousers, denim or a little black dress without adding a heel. Try a burgundy ballet flat with an otherwise black outfit, or pick a black Mary Jane if you want a shoe that will work with several party looks.

Start with the [shape that suits your wardrobe](#shapes). A ballet flat shows more of the foot, while a slipper usually covers more of the instep. The [five outfit formulas](#outfits) give each shape a place to start, and the [boots guide](#boots) can help if the walk is a bigger part of the evening than the outfit.

## Why velvet flats are showing up now

[Vogue’s Laura Jackson](https://www.vogue.com/article/velvet-flats-trend) describes velvet flats as part of a move toward richer finishes in everyday clothes. The fabric can look formal with a matching set and relaxed with a chunky sweater. [British Vogue](https://www.vogue.co.uk/article/velvet-ballet-flats-trend) highlights the ballet-flat version, while [Who What Wear’s Natalie Munro](https://www.whowhatwear.com/fashion/celebrity-style/velvet-loafers-trend-jennifer-lawrence-2026) points to Jennifer Lawrence wearing burgundy velvet flats with an all-black look.

## Four shapes to try

Shape
What it does in an outfit

Ballet flat
A low-cut upper leaves a line of skin or tights between the shoe and a cropped hem.

Velvet slipper
A higher vamp has a loafer-like silhouette. Try it with straight trousers so the shape remains visible.

Mary Jane
The strap becomes part of the outfit. It stands out against tights or under a shorter skirt.

Flat mule
The backless shape works when you want a relaxed line with a matching set or trousers. Check how the hem falls over the open heel.

Black is an easy first choice if several outfits share the same tights or tailoring. Burgundy or oxblood makes an especially good accent against charcoal, navy and black. A dusty pink or blue can echo a color in a scarf. If you like a stronger accent, try burnt orange against deep denim or royal purple beside a black skirt. If the goal is a more coordinated holiday look, see how other [velvet party pieces](https://fashion.allwomenstalk.com/voluptuous-holiday-party-looks-made-from-velvet/) use the fabric.

## Five outfits for velvet flats

### Jeans and a chunky sweater

Borrow the denim-and-knit combination Vogue suggests. Put burgundy or deep purple flats under dark straight jeans and wear a ribbed sweater with enough shape to balance the small shoe. Stand in the shoes and check whether the hem breaks over the entire toe. If it does, try a slightly shorter jean or turn the cuff once. A fitted jean also leaves room for a strap, as in these [skinny-jean outfits](https://allwomenstalk.com/skinny-jeans-are-back/).

### Tailored trousers and a winter coat

A dark velvet slipper works with straight charcoal or navy trousers and a long coat. Match the socks or tights to the trousers if you want a continuous line. Try taking off the coat in front of a mirror and check that the layers underneath feel finished. A button-front shirt or smooth knit adds shape without hiding the appeal of the shoes.

### A black blazer and one point of color

Use the Lawrence pairing as a starting point. Wear a black blazer, black top and black trousers with a mulberry or burgundy flat. If the trousers are wide, favor a visible toe or shorter hem so the point of color shows when you stand. A black bag leaves the shoe as the only accent.

### A little black dress with sheer tights

Jackson’s pairing of sheer tights and a black dress works with a black ballet flat or a Mary Jane. Choose the strap if you want a more defined outline at the foot and the low-cut flat if you want the tights to form a longer line. If you prefer trousers for the same party, try one of these [holiday party pants outfits](https://fashion.allwomenstalk.com/skip-the-dress-wear-these-trends-to-holiday-parties-instead/).

### A festive matching set

Wear a slim velvet flat with a coordinated knit top and skirt or a simple matching trouser set. You can match the shoe to the set or use a neighboring color, like burgundy with a rust-toned set. When the set already has a bold pattern, a solid deep-colored shoe provides a defined edge without adding a second print.

## When to take the boots

Who What Wear makes an exception for a particularly long walk. Think about the whole journey before getting dressed. If you have a long walk to the venue, wear the boots you planned to walk in and bring the flats to change into. If your plans involve going straight from the car or train to an indoor event, you may happily stay in flats all evening. ## Frequently asked questions

### Are velvet flats in style?

Yes. Vogue, British Vogue and Who What Wear have all featured them in winter fashion coverage. Ballet flats, slippers and Mary Janes offer distinct ways to wear the fabric.

### How do you wear velvet ballet flats with jeans?

Start with straight or slim jeans whose hem leaves the shape of the shoe visible. Try a chunky knit up top and repeat the color of the shoe only if it suits the outfit.

### What colors work for velvet flats?

Black and burgundy are versatile with dark winter clothes. Dusty blue, pink, purple and burnt orange work as accents against a quieter outfit.

### Can you wear velvet flats instead of boots in winter?

Yes, especially for indoor plans and a short trip to the venue. If you have a long walk, you can wear boots for the journey and change into flats when you arrive.

### What is the difference between a ballet flat and a velvet slipper?

A ballet flat usually has a lower-cut upper. A slipper generally covers more of the instep and has the outline of a soft loafer.
