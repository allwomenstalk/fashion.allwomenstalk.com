# 8 Attention\-Grabbing Statement Rings You Need to Try

Sometimes it is fun to wear a piece of jewelry that is completely outrageous. If you want to turn heads, wearing a big ring in a bold design can often do the trick. The latest runway trends highlight eye-catching, big chunky rings as essential accessories for elevating any outfit. Whether you want to draw ring attention to your hands or complement a sleek outfit, adding a statement piece creates an instant focal point. You might have seen them popping up in your favorite stores or online over the past couple of months. Here are 8 attention grabbing rings that you simply can’t miss.

## 4. Mother of Pearl Flower Ring

![Mother of Pearl Flower Ring](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2011/06/76808170-01_209x210.jpg)

**Price:** $5.80 at [forever21.com](https://www.forever21.com) You will look like a million bucks with this pretty flower ring. The white lacquered petals are simple and chic. The glittery crystals in the middle look expensive and glamorous, especially since they are set in silver. This ring is a bold way to spice up your look for the office or a night out with your partner.

Here are quick tips for styling floral jewelry:

- Pair with neutral outfits to make the petals pop.
- Keep other accessories minimal to avoid clutter.
- Wear on your index or middle finger for maximum impact.
