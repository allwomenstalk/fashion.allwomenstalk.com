# Embellished Sweaters Are the Cozy Holiday Party Look: How to Wear Them

An embellished sweater does the job of a party top with the ease of a knit. A few pearls around the collar can dress up dark jeans for a family dinner. A beaded cardigan and tailored black trousers can feel finished enough for a work party, even if you are coming straight from the office.

Start by deciding [how much sparkle suits the room](#sparkle). Then borrow one of these [five outfit formulas](#outfits), using the trousers, skirt or denim you already own. The shoes and bag can do most of the work of taking the same sweater from a casual dinner to a dressier invitation.

## What counts as an embellished sweater?

An embellished knit has decoration added to the garment, such as beads, crystals, sequins, pearls or embroidery. A pullover gives you one clean shape. A cardigan lets you decide whether the decorations sit around an open neckline or form a closed front. A sweatshirt with a dressy collar can work, too, especially with relaxed jeans or a simple skirt.

Placement changes the effect. Pearls along the collar draw attention toward your face, which is useful at a dinner where most of the outfit is below the table. Decoration along the cuffs shows when you pick up a glass. A fully sequined sweater makes the whole top the main feature, so the pieces below it can be very simple.

Fashion writers [Kelsey Stewart at Who What Wear](https://www.whowhatwear.com/fashion/jumpers-sweaters/best-embellished-sweaters) and [Gaby Keiderling at ELLE](https://www.elle.com/fashion/shopping/a74295929/best-embellished-sweaters-trend/) both cover decorated knits for the holidays. For a less conspicuous version, start with a pearl collar or a few stones at the neckline. If the piece you most want to wear is a [sequin skirt](https://fashion.allwomenstalk.com/how-to-style-a-sequin-skirt/), try it with an unembellished knit to give the skirt the attention.

## Five ways to wear an embellished sweater

### 1. Dark jeans for a family dinner

Both Who What Wear and ELLE pair a dressed-up knit with jeans. Try a pearl-trim cardigan with dark straight-leg jeans and simple flats. Let the sweater sit just above the waistband if you prefer a clean line. If yours is long, leave it loose over a slim straight leg. Check where the jean hem falls over your shoe before settling on the pair.

### 2. A satin midi for a cocktail party

ELLE suggests a satin midi skirt as a partner to an embellished knit. Put a small-scale beaded sweater over a charcoal or navy skirt and add a low heel. If you try a cropped cardigan, button it over a camisole and check how much of the waistband shows when you sit. A plain evening bag keeps the skirt’s sheen and the beads from competing for attention.

### 3. Fluid trousers for dinner out

Stewart suggests silk trousers with slingbacks, and Keiderling also makes a case for velvet trousers. Try a fitted silver-trim sweater with wide-leg navy trousers and a slingback. Bring the shoes when you try on the pants so you can see how the hem moves. For more alternatives to a dress, see these [holiday party trouser outfits](https://fashion.allwomenstalk.com/skip-the-dress-wear-these-trends-to-holiday-parties-instead/).

### 4. Leather for an informal evening party

For the leather pairing ELLE describes, keep the embellishment to a cuff or a border. A slightly boxy sweater over slim black pants gives each piece space, and tonal ankle boots carry the line down to the floor. If you prefer straight leather trousers, try a shorter cardigan so the top of the trousers is visible and the two shapes stay distinct.

### 5. Black tailoring for the office party

Stewart’s work-party pairing is black trousers and kitten heels. A small crystal collar is enough to make the outfit feel considered, especially with a pair of simple studs. If you are finishing a full day at the office first, use a blazer during the day and take it off when it is time for the party. Check the invitation’s [dress code](https://fashion.allwomenstalk.com/tips-for-deciphering-dress-codes-and-sticking-to-them/) before choosing denim or a sweatshirt.

## Put it on with the rest of your evening in mind

Try the sweater under the coat you will wear. A beaded cuff that disappears under the sleeve may be the right detail for indoors. Sequins around a shoulder can also change where you want to carry a bag, so test the strap before you leave. If the embellishment is concentrated around the collar, try earrings or a ring where the jewelry has its own space.

Follow the garment’s care label, including its instructions for washing and drying. Hand-wash or spot-clean only if the label allows it. A removable pin can come off before cleaning. Fold the sweater for storage if the garment’s guidance permits it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an embellished sweater?

It is a knit decorated with details such as pearls, beads, sequins, crystals or embroidery. The decoration may cover the front or appear only on the neckline, cuffs or hem.

### How do you style it for a holiday party?

Pair it with a bottom that suits the event, like dark denim, a satin midi or tailored trousers. Give a statement collar or all-over sequins room by keeping the jewelry simple.

### What pants go with a sparkly sweater?

Try dark straight-leg jeans, smooth black trousers or velvet pants. Hold the pieces together in the room where you are getting ready to see how their finishes look side by side.

### Can you wear it to an office party?

Yes, if the event’s dress code allows it. A lightly decorated sweater with black trousers and a low heel is an easy pairing.

### How do you clean a beaded sweater?

Use the garment’s care label. The right treatment depends on the knit and on how the decoration is attached.
