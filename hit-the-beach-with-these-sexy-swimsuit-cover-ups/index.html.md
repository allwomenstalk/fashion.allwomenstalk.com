# 42 Sexy Swimsuit Cover Ups for Your Ultimate Beach Style

Summer is right around the corner, which means it is time to refresh your swimwear wardrobe. Even when you feel completely confident in your favorite swimsuit, having a stylish, comfortable beach cover up is essential for transitioning from the sand to the boardwalk. The best sexy swimsuit cover ups offer the perfect balance of effortless style, sun protection, and breezy comfort.

| Style Category | Key Feature | Best Occasion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sheer & Lace | Lightweight & delicate | Poolside relaxation |
| Boho Sweaters & Knits | Cozy texture | Sunset beach walks |
| Bold Prints & Kaftans | Full coverage & vibrant | Resort & vacation |

When picking the perfect cover up for summer, keep these quick tips in mind:

- Choose lightweight, fast-drying fabrics like chiffon, cotton lace, or rayon.

- Opt for versatile pieces that double as casual daytime outfits.

- Pick colors and cuts that showcase your unique personal style.
