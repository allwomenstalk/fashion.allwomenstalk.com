# 10 Stylish Refashion Ideas from A Pair and a Spare to Upgrade Your Wardrobe

Stylish refashion ideas are always a fun and sustainable way to update your look without breaking the bank. They offer incredible inspiration for anyone on a shopping ban or looking to get creative with their existing wardrobe. When it comes to **stylish refashion ideas**, few creators are as innovative as Geneva from A Pair & a Spare. Geneva advocates for a sustainable approach to fashion, turning older garments into chic, on-trend pieces through sewing, studding, and creative restyling. Below are ten **awesome and stylish refashion ideas** shared by Geneva to help refresh your wardrobe.

Essential supplies for wardrobe refashioning projects:

- Fabric shears & measuring tape

- Fabric glue or basic sewing kit

- Upcycled thrifted garments & trim

## 1. Neon-Lined Lace Mini Skirt

![Neon-Lined Lace Mini Skirt](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/05/1_640x428.jpg)

Neon accents continue to make a bold statement in modern streetwear. If you prefer a subtle hint of bright color rather than an all-over neon outfit, this **stylish refashion idea** is perfect for you. Check out Geneva's fab tutorial on how to line a lace skirt with bright neon lining for a striking pop of color underneath standard lace.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2012/02/diy-brightly-lined-lace-mini-skirt.html)

## 2. Scarf to Kimono

![Scarf to Kimono](https://img.allw.mn/content/fashion/2012/05/2_583x472.png)

Got a pretty scarf that you've never worn? Save it from the donate pile by making this fab kimono top. This idea is also great for making a fabulous beach cover-up to wear layered over a statement piece like a [sequin bra](#10).

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2011/08/diy-tribal-print-kimono.html)

## 3. Jason Wu-Inspired Lace Jeans

![Jason Wu-Inspired Lace Jeans](https://img.allw.mn/content/fashion/2012/05/3_640x465.png)

Those designer pieces with scalloped lace details from luxury brands like [Jason Wu](https://www.jasonwustudio.com) are pretty awesome, but they can be quite pricey. You can stop saving up for a designer tag because this **stylish refashion idea** shows you how to channel Jason Wu's signature lace details at home. According to Geneva, "the scalloped lace gives a gorgeous and on-trend look, and a friend mentioned when I was wearing them that the lace was quite flattering on the legs, um, score!" Score indeed.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2011/08/j-brand-collaboration-jason-wu-inspired.html)

## 4. Ombre Tee

![Ombre Tee](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/05/4_640x480.jpg)

It's not too late to join the ombre trend. If you can't dare to do it to your hair, doing it to an old white tee is the next best thing to give casual basic clothing a fresh gradient effect.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2011/12/diy-bec-bridge-inspired-ombre-tee.html)

## 5. Printed Cut out Dress

![Printed Cut out Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/05/5_1024x768.jpg)

Cut out dresses are a perennial favorite for warm spring and summer days. One cheap and creative way to channel the trend is to do a **stylish refashion** using an old patterned skirt. For this project, you will need a midi skirt with a fun print. You will also need lots of sunny days so that you can show off your creation.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2012/01/before-after-printed-cut-out-dress.html)

## 6. Spiked Heels

![Spiked Heels](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/05/6_640x480.jpg)

These look ridiculously awesome. If you have a pair of suede pumps that need a makeover, adding metallic spikes is exactly what you need to do for instant edgy style.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2012/02/diy-spiked-heels.html)

## 7. Michael Kors-Inspired Cut out Dress

![Michael Kors-Inspired Cut out Dress](https://img.allw.mn/content/fashion/2012/05/7_602x435.png)

Here is another cut out **refashion idea** that any high fashion-loving fashionista will love. The inspiration behind this DIY project is the iconic red-carpet silhouette from [Michael Kors](https://www.michaelkors.com) worn by Miranda Kerr to the CFDA awards. You can achieve a similar high-fashion waist-cutout effect on a budget using thrifted dresses.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2011/08/diy-miranda-kerr-michael-kors-inspired.html)

## 8. Peplum Skirt

![Peplum Skirt](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/05/8_1024x768.jpg)

Looking for a **refashion idea** that will give your wardrobe an instant trend update? Then this peplum skirt tutorial is for you. Using thrifted items and basic sewing skills, you can make this skirt in no time and pair it easily with [custom lace jeans](#3) or basic tops.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2012/02/diy-peplum-skirt.html)

Best thrift store finds for DIY clothing projects:

- Oversized printed silk scarves for kimono transformations

- Structured midi skirts for customized cut-out dresses

- Classic neutral heels ready for stud or spike embellishments

## 9. Fishtail Skirt

![Fishtail Skirt](https://img.allw.mn/content/fashion/2012/05/9_640x517.png)

Here is another **stylish refashion** idea that stays continuously on trend. Inspired by bohemian aesthetic labels like [Free People](https://www.freepeople.com), this tutorial is very easy to follow. It is affordable too, requiring only a simple thrifted skirt and basic hemlining technique.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2011/10/diy-fishtail-skirt-free-people.html)

## 10. Sequin Bra

![Sequin Bra](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/05/10_640x480.jpg)

Going to a beach party or festive event? Turn your chic factor up a notch by making this fabulous sequin bra. Wear under a sheer top or relaxed cover-up like the [scarf kimono](#2) so you stay stylish and comfortable.

[Tutorial](http://apair-andaspare.blogspot.com/2012/02/diy-sequin-bra.html)

Geneva's creative projects demonstrate how accessible DIY style can be. Beyond her ingenuity, these **stylish refashion ideas** require simple steps, remain wallet-friendly, and help recreate designer looks effortlessly.
