# 7 Grown\-Up Ways to Wear Gingham: How to Style the Trend

If you are asking yourself **is gingham in style** this season, the answer is a resounding yes. This classic checked pattern has evolved far beyond picnic blankets and childhood dresses into a major fashion statement. Finding chic **how to wear gingham** ideas that feel sophisticated and modern can transform your wardrobe. Whether you want to try a subtle hint of the **gingham trend** or go all-in with bold patterns, here are 7 grown-up ways to style this timeless print with confidence.

## 1. Supersize It

![Supersize It](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/nf/fa/bp/ysnsofxe_572x768.jpg)

One of the most stylish ways to wear gingham right now is in oversized prints. Exaggerated and oversized gingham prints have been dominating top designer collections featured in [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com). Jumbo check squares offer a playful twist while making an instant statement. These larger-scale checks feel fresh and elevated compared to traditional small-check patterns of the past.

## 2. Keep It Tailored

![Keep It Tailored](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/0u/dt/k7/panzjqqj_600x917.jpg)

If you’re planning a full head-to-toe pattern outfit, sharp tailoring keeps the ensemble looking polished and adult. Avoid anything overly slouchy or voluminous that might look unstructured. If you love the dramatic scale of [oversized gingham prints](#1), structured silhouettes keep the look balanced and refined.

Key tailored pieces to look for include:

- Structured blazers with crisp shoulders
- High-waisted fitted trousers
- Sleek pencil skirts and sheath dresses

## 3. Make It Menswear-inspired

![Make It Menswear-inspired](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/a7/bb/i6/j2s7xeh0_600x900.jpg)

Give your outfit a sophisticated edge by embracing a menswear-inspired look. Team tailored gingham trousers with polished loafers or lace-up leather shoes and a fitted shirt for a smart and casual outfit. This feminine meets masculine way of dressing is sleek, modern, and one of the top ways to pull off the trend this season.

## 4. Add Accents

![Add Accents](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/lp/pq/cf/jfjixbdu_600x899.jpg)

Gingham accessories make for great accent pieces. They’re also a fantastic option for when you only want to add a touch of gingham to your outfit. Gingham shoes, bags, or even gingham-framed sunglasses are all stylish ways to incorporate this trend into your look. Opt for brightly colored gingham accessories that will add a playful twist to your outfit.

| Accessory Type | Best Styling Pair | Vibe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gingham Pumps | Solid Slip Dress | Chic & Retro |
| Printed Tote | Linen Trousers & Tee | Summer Casual |
| Check Scarf | Neutral Trench Coat | Subtle Accent |

## 5. Toughen It up

![Toughen It up](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fe/0e/vo/n1ou2y5y_421x576.jpg)

If your gingham clothes take the form of sweet skirts or smock dresses, try toughening up the rest of your outfit. You could try teaming a gingham dress with a leather-look biker jacket, slouchy beanie, and ankle boots for a contemporary take on the trend. Also try accessorising with chunky jewellery and punk-inspired pieces to really up the ante. To contrast with [menswear-inspired tailoring](#3), try pairing delicate checks with edgy leather or denim layers.

## 6. Make It Monochrome

Gingham prints can be quite busy so it’s best to stick to monochrome colour palettes. Black and white gingham prints are probably the sleekest and most modern of them all, and the easiest to accessorise. If you’re wearing coloured gingham, try keeping the rest of your outfit in the same tonal colours to make the look more streamlined. Avoid accessories that vary greatly in colour and opt for matching accessories in similar tones.

## 7. Go Sheer

![Go Sheer](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/xa/za/ju/qphk146b_460x575.jpg)

Gingham goes sexy when worn in sheer styles. Sheer fabrics have a way of sticking around each season, and even the classic gingham print isn’t exempt from being given the sheer treatment. A sheer gingham blouse can look super sleek when tucked into a fitted pencil skirt and worn with polished heels and accessories. Alternatively, floaty sheer gingham dresses can be toughened up with structured jackets and heeled ankle boots. Pairing sheer tops with [monochrome palettes](#6) creates a polished, sophisticated look.

The key to styling gingham this season is to stick to modern silhouettes. These are just some tips for working the gingham trend in a more grown up manner. Are you a fan of gingham? What are your top tips for wearing the trend?
