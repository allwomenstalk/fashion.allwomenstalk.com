# 7 Pretty & Cute Holiday Dresses for Every Party

One of the best things about the holiday season is all of the cute holiday dresses you can wear! From delicate lace to dazzling sparkles, finding pretty holiday dresses makes attending winter parties so much fun. Whether you are looking for a subtle classic or nice holiday dresses that turn heads, the options are endless. So amidst the tons of options you can try, here are a few of my favorite cute holiday dress styles for this holiday season. And don’t forget to accessorize!

## 1. ASOS Lace Skater Dress

![ASOS Lace Skater Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/12/1_asos-lace-skater-dress_600x766.jpg)

This dress from [ASOS](https://www.asos.com) has all of my qualifications for cute holiday dresses. It features a cute collar, intricate lace, and of course a little bit of sparkle. This is the perfect dress to wear if you are feeling fun and flirty. Pair it with some gold accessories and a pair of nude heels. And don’t forget to put your hair in an updo to show off that rockin’ collar. If you want something with even more shine later in the night, you can also check out a [sparkly party dress](#5) for extra glamour.

## 2. MINKPINK Outrageous Fortune Dress

![MINKPINK Outrageous Fortune Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/12/2_minkpink-outrageous-fortune-dress_600x900.jpg)

Embrace that ever so hot baroque trend this season with this adorable dress available at [Urban Outfitters](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com). Put an edgy spin on the trend with a zipper going down the front of the dress and a cutout in the back. Add to the edginess with a pair of black studded heels and a thick black leather bracelet. This is a great choice when looking for nice holiday dresses that bring out your inner badass.

- Pair structured cuts with edgy metallic accents.
- Keep footwear bold with studded pumps or dark boots.
- Choose understated hair to let back cutouts stand out.

## 3. Blazing Beauty Dress

![Blazing Beauty Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/12/3_blazing-beauty-dress_350x500.jpg)

Look red hot in the snow with this long sleeve lace getup, available at [ModCloth](https://www.modcloth.com). It is perfect to stand out at any holiday party without feeling like you are going overboard. This dress would be perfect with big vintage curls and some understated accessories. If you want a warmer option with rich texture, consider a [burgundy velvet skater dress](#6) as well.

## 4. Ribbed Peplum Pencil Dress

![Ribbed Peplum Pencil Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/12/4_ribbed-peplum-pencil-dress_600x900.jpg)

Who says you can’t wear white in winter? Embrace the ever so popular peplum trend in this white peplum cocktail dress. This cute holiday dress is the perfect pick for anyone who loves to express themselves through accessories and shoes. Because it is such a clean and simple silhouette, feel free to dress it up anyway you want with some bold accessories.

## 5. Stardust Sequin Dress

![Stardust Sequin Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/12/5_stardust-sequin-dress_600x900.jpg)

If you want more adventurous cute holiday dresses, try shining in this hot sparkly party dress from [Nasty Gal](https://www.nastygal.com). It is just what you need to feel flirty at any celebration. Pair it with gold accessories and voluminous hair to really add to your shine. And don’t forget to add in some gold sky-high heels. For a more tailored alternative, you can also look back at a [white peplum cocktail dress](#4).

## 6. Maalika Long Sleeve Velvet Skater Dress

![Maalika Long Sleeve Velvet Skater Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/12/6_maalika-long-sleeve-velvet-skater-dress_355x520.jpg)

What is so great about this burgundy velvet skater dress is that you can go glam or edgy with it. Plus, it's affordable and versatile. Dress it up with some sparkly bling accessories and over the top hair. Or you can add a military jacket and studded boots for a more casual, laid back look. No matter what, you will look smoking hot when searching for pretty holiday dresses this season.

## 7. Brocade Bow Tube Dress

![Brocade Bow Tube Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/12/7_brocade-bow-tube-dress_328x400.jpg)

This may be my obsession with bows, but I love this gold bow dress. Go for a girly feel and style your hair in a cute updo. Pair that with dainty jewelry and patterned black tights and black pumps. You will be sure to turn heads in this feminine frock.
