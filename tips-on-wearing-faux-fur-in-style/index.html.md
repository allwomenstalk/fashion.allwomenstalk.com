# How to Wear a Faux Fur Jacket: 7 Chic Winter Styling Rules

It’s that time of the year where my favorite shops fill up with plush faux fur. With fashion icons regularly stepping out in cozy statement textures, mastering how to wear a faux fur jacket or accessory has become an essential winter skill. Faux fur is comfortable, warm, and ideal for transforming an outfit from day to night. However, to get it right, make sure you steer clear of novelty items and dodgy prints, opting for chic and elegant pieces instead. Want to elevate your cold-weather wardrobe effortlessly? Here’s how to wear faux fur the right way.

## 1. Get Gloved

![Get Gloved](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/11/13_900x587.jpg)

Everyone has a selection of thick tights and wooly jumpers for cool days, and these often look great teamed with faux fur accessories! Adding a pair of plush fur gloves to your look is a really easy way to keep yourself warmer while staying stylish. Plain black gloves are a timeless choice that will go with anything, or you can opt for gloves featuring a subtle monochrome pattern to make them eye-catching. If you are wondering [how to wear a faux fur gilet](#3) alongside smaller accents, starting with trim details on your gloves creates a cohesive silhouette.

Here are a few quick tips when selecting cold-weather gloves:

- Choose touchscreen-compatible faux fur trim gloves for convenience.

- Pair neutral gloves with colorful winter coats for subtle contrast.

- Opt for fleece-lined interiors to maximize warmth.

## 2. Go Vintage

![Go Vintage](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/11/23_600x800.jpg)

Full faux fur coats aren't always practical in unexpected weather. I learned this the hard way when my snuggly warm coat got drenched in a sudden rainstorm. Don’t make the same mistake! Instead, choose a tailored coat made of thick wool and elevate it with a faux fur collar and cuffs. For instance, a classic [brown wool coat outfit](#1) accented with detachable fur trim delivers vintage glamour without the risk of heavy water damage. Matching the trim to your coat gives it a tailored feel, or you can choose a coordinating color to add rich detail. For more dramatic evening looks, you can even explore a dramatic fur coat with train accents on high-fashion runways featured on [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com).

## 3. Home County Style

![Home County Style](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/11/33_396x594.jpg)

Until recently, gilets seemed best suited strictly for country walks, but a tailored gilet can be a standout urban wardrobe piece. A light gray gilet matches almost everything, pairing effortlessly with everything from casual denim to flowy maxi dresses. It’s soft, warm, and easy to layer under thicker coats on extra chilly days. Every winter wardrobe benefits from a retro-inspired faux fur gilet.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Faux Fur Gilet | Sleeveless & Easy Layering | Casual Weekend Outings |
| Trimmed Wool Coat | Structured & Tailored | Work & Formal Evenings |
| Fur-Lined Parka | Heavy-Duty Weather Resistance | Travel & Outdoor Snow Days |

## 4. Jumper Joy

![Jumper Joy](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/11/43_486x730.jpg)

Sweaters and jumpers are winter staples, and adding fur accents creates the perfect cozy statement piece. Avoid jumpers made completely of faux fur, as they can be tricky to wash and maintain properly. Instead, look for knitwear with faux fur collars or sleeve detailing. Cable knit styles look gorgeous worn over dark skater dresses paired with [knee high boots](#6). Thinner button-up jumpers go great with pencil skirts for a sophisticated office outfit.

## 5. Check out Fur Lining

![Check out Fur Lining](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/11/52_490x735.jpg)

If fur collars and gloves aren’t enough, it’s time to invest in a fur-lined jacket. These usually feature a weather-resistant material on the outside and a thick fur lining to keep you insulated on the inside. Shearling jackets remain a favorite choice, offering versatility across neutral tones like beige, caramel, and cream. Knowing **how to wear a faux fur jacket** with soft interior lining ensures you stay warm without feeling overly bulky.

## 6. Keep Your Toes Cozy

![Keep Your Toes Cozy](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/11/62_423x635.jpg)

You lose a lot of heat through your feet, so having quality thermal socks ready for winter is essential. Opt for thick designs and don’t hesitate to pick higher sock cuts. Knee-high socks are incredibly warm and cozy throughout the colder months. Ankle socks with a fluffy top trim are also great for adding subtle texture above your ankle boots. If you need maximum insulation, fur-lined snow boots provide waterproof protection and plush comfort for winter walking.

## 7. Heads up!

![Heads up!](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fashion/2012/11/72_870x1110.jpg)

Finally, don’t forget cozy fur headwear and accessories. If you love hats, options range from sleek Russian-style pillbox hats to relaxed beanies with faux fur pom-poms. Furry headwarmers and ear warmers are another fantastic invention—that soft band of fur keeps your ears protected against cold winds while keeping your hairstyle intact. If you ever need guidance on maintaining your plush accessories, check out general fabric care guidelines on [wikiHow](https://www.wikihow.com).

Once you’ve worked out how to wear faux fur in a way that fits your personal style, staying warm during winter becomes effortless. Refreshing your cold-weather rotation with plush textures makes layering fun and stylish. Remember to check care labels carefully so you know how to clean your garments properly without damaging the fibers.
